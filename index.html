📞 Still Have Questions?
How do I get started with filing my ITR? +
Simply call us or WhatsApp, share your Form 16 / bank statements / income details, and our CA will handle the rest. Filing is completed within 24–48 hours.
Is my personal and financial data safe with you? +
Absolutely. All data is shared only with your assigned CA, stored on encrypted servers, and never shared with third parties. We follow strict data confidentiality protocols.
What documents do I need for GST registration? +
PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details, business address proof, and a passport-size photo. Our CA will guide you through the exact checklist based on your business type.
Do you handle notice replies for previous years? +
Yes. We handle notices for any assessment year, including older ones. Our senior CAs have resolved hundreds of 143(1), 148, and show-cause notices successfully.
What if I'm an NRI — can I still use your services? +
Yes! We serve NRIs across the globe for Indian income tax filing, DTAA compliance, and repatriation of funds. Everything is handled 100% online.
How do I pay? Are online payments accepted? +
We accept UPI, Net Banking, NEFT/IMPS, and cards. Payment is made only after you're fully satisfied with the service quote and scope. No advance required for consultations.
