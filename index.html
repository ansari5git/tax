Common Questions Answered

Everything you need before getting started with us.

How do I start filing my ITR?+
Call or WhatsApp us, share your Form 16 or income details, and our CA handles the rest. Filing is done in 24–48 hours with acknowledgment sent directly to you.
Is my financial data safe with you?+
Absolutely. Your data is shared only with your assigned CA, stored on encrypted servers, and never sold or shared with any third party under any circumstance.
What documents do I need for GST registration?+
PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details, business address proof, and a passport photo. Your CA will send a precise checklist based on your business type after a quick call.
Can you handle notices for previous years?+
Yes. We handle notices for any assessment year. Our senior CAs have resolved hundreds of 143(1), 148, and show-cause notices across all types and years.
I'm an NRI — can I use your services?+
Absolutely. We serve NRIs globally for Indian income tax filing, DTAA benefits, and repatriation. Everything is handled 100% online with no physical presence needed.
How do I pay? Is online payment accepted?+
We accept UPI, Net Banking, NEFT/IMPS, and cards. No advance for consultations — you pay only after agreeing on the scope and quote with your CA.
What if you miss a filing deadline?+
If we miss a deadline due to our error, we pay the late fee — not you. This commitment is part of our GST return filing plan and reflects our confidence in our process.
